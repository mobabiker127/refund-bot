# About Refund Bot

Refund Bot was originally created to help maintain moderation and to create a fun experience in my personal Discord community, which is a server I co-owned from 2020 until 2022 with around 200 active members. The goal was to automate moderation tasks, streamline communication, and make enjoyable interactive commands for everyone involved. Over my journey of developing this bot I learned much more than from what I initially thought was a copy and paste task from a few tutorial videos. I learned about the importance of constant uptime, dealing with issues, and most importantly, reflecting on feedback received from the community and dealing with it responsibly.

I initially created this bot in my early stages of my university degree, which was during the lockdown period of COVID-19. I spent most of my time learning about keeping a server running 24/7, looking at databases and how each user's data will be stored, and coming up with ideas to make a more exciting experience from this bot. There were many challenges that came to face me, but I didn't let these stop my motivation for learning about how everything worked & keeping up to community expectations. I loved the aspect of being able to interact with everyone in this way, which increased my motivation to pursue my degree in CompSci.

Looking forward, I aim to keep this bot as a symbol of my early stages of my path as a programmer.
